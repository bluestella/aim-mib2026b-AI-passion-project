## Subject

Request for Materials Recovery Facility inventory and LGU compliance status, Cainta, Rizal

## Request body

> Good day,
>
> Under Executive Order No. 2, s. 2016, I respectfully request copies of the following
> records held by the Environmental Management Bureau, Region IV-A:
>
> 1. The current **inventory of Materials Recovery Facilities (MRFs)** in the
>    Municipality of Cainta, Province of Rizal, including for each facility its name or
>    identifier, barangay, address or coordinates, operational status, and the waste
>    streams it accepts.
>
> 2. The most recent **RA 9003 compliance status** recorded for the Municipality of
>    Cainta, including the number of barangays reported to have organised Barangay Solid
>    Waste Management Committees and the number practising segregation at source.
>
> 3. Any regional monitoring report or bulletin from CY 2023 onward that reports solid
>    waste diversion rates for the LGUs of Rizal province.
>
> A spreadsheet or CSV would be ideal if the inventory is held in that form.
>
> **Purpose.** I am building a free, non-commercial web application for Cainta residents
> that shows where a given item of household waste can be recovered locally. Accurate
> MRF locations are needed so the application directs people to real facilities rather
> than to guesses. Any figure published from these records will carry an explicit
> citation to the source document and its date.
>
> Thank you for your assistance.

## Why this request

The national MRF count is public (12,864 nationally as of 2025, per plan Appendix A),
but the national aggregate cannot route a user to a facility. Plan §3 (Tier 2.12)
identifies the regional EMB office as the level where facility-specific records
actually live.

This request also cross-checks Request A: if NSWMC and EMB R4A report different MRF
counts for Cainta, that discrepancy is itself a finding worth knowing before quoting
either number.

## Expected outcome

| Outcome | Probability | What it unlocks |
|---|---|---|
| MRF inventory with locations | Medium | Recovery nodes for the Pathway Card, independent of OSM's sparse coverage |
| Compliance status only | Medium | Context for the leakage narrative; no routing value |
| Referral to the LGU | Medium–high | Same door as Request C, approached from a different direction |
| Denial or no response | Low–medium | OSM seed plus field survey still carries Phase 2 |

## Notes on scope

MRF locations are facility records, not personal data. If any response withholds
coordinates on privacy grounds, barangay-level granularity is an acceptable fallback —
say so in a follow-up rather than appealing.

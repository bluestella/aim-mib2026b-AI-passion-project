## Subject

Request for Cainta, Rizal solid waste management reporting records (SCMAR and 10-Year SWM Plan)

## Request body

> Good day,
>
> Under Executive Order No. 2, s. 2016, I respectfully request copies of the following
> records held by the National Solid Waste Management Commission concerning the
> **Municipality of Cainta, Province of Rizal (PSGC 0405805000)**:
>
> 1. The most recent **LGU Solid Waste Management Status and Compliance Monitoring and
>    Assessment Report (SCMAR)** submitted by or for the Municipality of Cainta,
>    covering any reporting period from CY 2020 to the present.
>
> 2. The **approved 10-Year Solid Waste Management Plan** of the Municipality of Cainta,
>    including the date of NSWMC approval and any subsequent amendments.
>
> 3. Any record of the Municipality of Cainta's **compliance status** under RA 9003,
>    including the number and locations of Materials Recovery Facilities (MRFs) reported
>    as operational, and the number of barangays with organised Barangay Solid Waste
>    Management Committees.
>
> If the records exist in a machine-readable format (spreadsheet or CSV), I would be
> grateful to receive them in that format. Scanned PDF is acceptable if that is the
> only form on file.
>
> **Purpose.** I am developing a free, non-commercial web application that helps Cainta
> residents identify where a given item of household waste should go — which materials
> can be sold or recovered locally, and which cannot. The application is intended to
> support, not audit, the municipality's RA 9003 compliance. Any figure derived from
> these records will be published with an explicit citation to the source document and
> its date.
>
> I understand the agency has 15 working days to respond, extendable once under Section
> 9 of the Order. Thank you for your assistance.

## Why this request

Per plan §3 (Tier 2.1), the SCMAR form is the canonical schema for what a Philippine
LGU is legally required to hold on solid waste. The blank form is public; the *filled*
Cainta submission is not. This request is the only route to it short of the MENRO.

## Expected outcome

| Outcome | Probability | What it unlocks |
|---|---|---|
| Full SCMAR received | Low–medium | Barangay-level compliance and MRF data — the single richest local dataset available |
| 10-Year Plan only | Medium | Collection structure, planned diversion targets |
| Referral to Cainta MENRO | Medium | A named contact and a documented reason to approach them — valuable in itself |
| Denial or no response | Medium | Costs nothing; Phases 2–3 do not depend on this |

## Notes on scope

Deliberately narrow. A request that asks for everything invites a denial for being
unreasonably burdensome. Three named documents for one named municipality is easy for
a records officer to act on.

Nothing here asks for personal data, so no privacy exception should apply.
